# Drill Library

Approved drills for use in workout design. Do not prescribe drills outside this list
without explicit coach approval. New drills are added by Ben.

**Tiers**: S = Essential | A = Great | B = Situational | C = Overrated — use sparingly

In sets, always write: `drill [Drill Name]` so the Commit parser recognises it as a drill set.

---

## Freestyle

**Fist Drill** | Tier: S
Equipment: None
Focus: Forearm feel / EVF awareness
Cues: Swim with closed fists; forearm becomes catch surface; alternate lengths fist / open hand
Notes: S tier: no equipment, any level, immediate impact on feel. Good warm-up activation drill.

**Single-Arm Freestyle** | Tier: A
Equipment: Fins recommended, snorkel opt.
Focus: EVF catch, full rotation
Cues: Working arm pulls, other arm stays at side (shoulder dry); rotate to breathe to working side
Notes: Arm-at-side variation only — A tier. Arm-extended variation is C tier and should not be used.

**Skulling** | Tier: A
Equipment: None
Focus: Feel for the water, catch pressure
Cues: Small sculling figure-8 with hands; can be done on front, side, or in reverse
Notes: More advanced — not for beginners. Multiple variations. Good in most workouts once mastered.

**3-Stroke + 12 Kicks** | Tier: A
Equipment: Fins optional
Focus: Body rotation, side balance
Cues: 3 strokes then balance on side for 12 kicks; keep hips and shoulder stacked
Notes: Can overemphasise rotation if overdone. Fins make it easier and more effective.

**Paddle on Head** | Tier: A
Equipment: Paddle
Focus: Breathing mechanics, head rotation
Cues: Balance paddle on head; rotate to breathe keeping one eye/ear submerged
Notes: Builds breathing confidence. Somewhat advanced. Fun drill with clear immediate feedback.

**Zipper Drill** | Tier: B
Equipment: None
Focus: High-elbow recovery
Cues: Thumb zips up torso into armpit on recovery; elbow leads high
Notes: Situational — good for most but not for sprinters using straighter arm recovery. Don't overdo.

**Fingertip Drag** | Tier: B
Equipment: None
Focus: High-elbow recovery, controlled glide
Cues: Fingertips drag along water surface on recovery; elbow leads
Notes: Can slow turnover and reduce rotation if overemphasised. Use situationally.

**Underwater Freestyle** | Tier: B
Equipment: Fins
Focus: Full rotation, body awareness
Cues: Swim full freestyle stroke completely submerged; maintain rotation and propulsion
Notes: Difficult — body buoyancy works against you. Fins required. Builds rotation and confidence.

**Bow and Arrow** | Tier: B
Equipment: Fins recommended
Focus: Side balance, high elbow
Cues: Balance on side for 6 kicks; arm out of water fingertips at 45° to surface
Notes: Harder variation of switch drill. Fins strongly recommended.

**Catchup Drill** | Tier: C
Equipment: None (fins optional)
Focus: Timing, EVF setup
Cues: Lead hand stays extended until trailing hand catches up at front
Notes: C tier — overrated. Overtraining this timing can hurt stroke. Use sparingly.

**3-3-3 Pause Drill** | Tier: A
Equipment: None
Focus: Front quadrant timing, stroke count
Cues: Three strokes, pause with both hands extended, repeat
Notes: Builds awareness of dead spots in the stroke cycle.

## Breaststroke

**Heel Tag** | Tier: S
Equipment: None
Focus: Kick mechanics, heel recovery
Cues: Hands under butt; tag heel on every kick; on stomach or back
Notes: Kick is 70-80% of breaststroke propulsion. S tier — no equipment, immediate feedback.

**Countdown Breaststroke** | Tier: S
Equipment: None
Focus: DPS, stroke efficiency, timing
Cues: Count strokes per 25; reduce by 1 each rep; work down then back up
Notes: S+ in video. 8x25 on generous rest. Forces stroke lengthening and timing awareness. Race pace effort.

**Two Kick One Pull** | Tier: A
Equipment: None
Focus: Streamline, DPS, stroke timing
Cues: Two kicks in streamline per pull; emphasise glide and length
Notes: Good for lengthening stroke and maximising DPS. Maintains stroke rhythm.

**Breast Pull with Fly Kick** | Tier: B
Equipment: Pull buoy optional, fins help
Focus: Pull isolation, hip-driven timing
Cues: Breaststroke arms with dolphin kick; time drive forward to streamline with powerful kick
Notes: B tier — difficult without fins. Activates core. Removes breaststroke kick to isolate arm timing.

**Breast Pull with Flutter Kick** | Tier: B
Equipment: None (fins optional)
Focus: Pull isolation, explosive timing
Cues: Breaststroke arms with flutter kick; be explosive; need to be fresh
Notes: B tier — more explosive than fly kick variation. Don't do daily — needs to be done fresh.

**Three Kick One Pull** | Tier: B
Equipment: None
Focus: Streamline, DPS
Cues: Three kicks in streamline per pull; exaggerates glide
Notes: B tier — similar to two kick one pull but breaks rhythm more. Good DPS drill.

**Vertical Kicking** | Tier: B
Equipment: None (medicine ball optional)
Focus: Leg strength, kick mechanics
Cues: Tread water using breaststroke kick; keep head above surface; vary hand position
Notes: B tier — fun and creative but hard to maintain full kick range of motion vertically.

**Breaststroke Kick Glide** | Tier: B
Equipment: None
Focus: Kick mechanics, streamline, glide
Cues: Kick then hold streamline glide; focus on body position and distance per kick
Notes: Emphasises the glide phase and streamline position after the kick.

**Single Arm Opposite Leg** | Tier: C
Equipment: None
Focus: Grip strength, feel for water
Cues: Grab opposite leg behind back; swim with one arm and one leg (e.g. right arm, left leg)
Notes: C tier — difficult with limited benefit. Better options exist for same adaptation.

## Backstroke

**Single-Arm Backstroke** | Tier: A
Equipment: None
Focus: Rotation, catch
Cues: One arm pulls, other at side; shoulder clears water on recovery

**Water Bottle on Head** | Tier: S
Equipment: Water bottle or cup
Focus: Head position, balance
Cues: Balance object on forehead; swim backstroke without dropping it; look straight up
Notes: S tier — teaches correct head position immediately. Fun drill with clear feedback. Any level.

**Six Kick Switch** | Tier: A
Equipment: Fins optional
Focus: Body rotation, side balance, length
Cues: 6 kicks on side with arm extended; transition with one full arm stroke to other side
Notes: A tier — do daily. Two variations: arm in stroke position or both arms at side. Also benefits freestyle.

**Backstroke Pull with Dolphin Kick** | Tier: A
Equipment: None
Focus: Tempo, hip drive, avoiding laziness
Cues: One dolphin kick per arm pull; high intensity short bursts; drive tempo and rotation
Notes: A tier — combats lazy backstroke. High energy drill, short durations only.

**Spin Drill** | Tier: B
Equipment: None
Focus: Arm tempo, turnover rate
Cues: Spin arms as fast as possible like a windmill; prop body up; high intensity
Notes: B tier — builds tempo but pushes body position low. Use for tempo awareness, not technique.

**Double Arm Backstroke** | Tier: B
Equipment: None
Focus: Shallow pull mechanics, body position
Cues: Both arms pull simultaneously; pinkies enter together; no rotation; keep kick going
Notes: B tier — flattens stroke to train shallower pull. Pair with rotation work for balance.

**Reverse Skulling on Back** | Tier: B
Equipment: None
Focus: Feel for water overhead, core stability
Cues: Float on back, arms overhead; scull with feet leading; body flat and level
Notes: B tier — more complex than standard skulling. Builds feel for catch position in backstroke.

**Backstroke Catchup Drill** | Tier: C
Equipment: Fins recommended
Focus: Catch timing, core stability
Cues: One arm always vertical in water; alternate when fingertips reach 90°
Notes: C tier — complex, requires fins and strong swimmer. Builds catch timing awareness.

**Backstroke with Paddles** | Tier: C
Equipment: Paddles
Focus: Pull strength
Cues: Full backstroke with paddles; focus on maintaining technique
Notes: C tier — injury risk to shoulders. Doesn't help underwater dolphin kick. Advanced only.

## Butterfly

**Flow Drill** | Tier: S
Equipment: None (snorkel/fins optional)
Focus: Full body undulation, core stability
Cues: On stomach, eyes down, fingertips just below surface; undulate entire body; kick up and down equally
Notes: S+ in video. Total body drill. Teaches correct undulation for fly and underwater dolphin. Any level.

**Dolphin Kick on Back** | Tier: S
Equipment: Fins optional
Focus: Core strength, undulation, hip drive
Cues: Streamline on back, eyes up; pop hips out of water; minimal knee bend; feel like a dolphin
Notes: S tier. Builds core, hip flexors, quads. Can be done with or without fins.

**333 Drill** | Tier: S
Equipment: None
Focus: Stroke balance, timing, undulation
Cues: 3 strokes right arm / 3 strokes left arm / 3 strokes both; breathe to side on single-arm portions
Notes: S tier. No equipment needed. Balances stroke and builds full undulation rhythm.

**Butterfly with Fins** | Tier: A
Equipment: Fins
Focus: Full stroke technique, endurance
Cues: Swim full butterfly with fins; focus on timing and undulation
Notes: A tier — fins extend duration and improve technique. Don't become dependent on fins.

**Head First Dolphin Kick** | Tier: A
Equipment: Fins recommended
Focus: Undulation, dolphin kick rhythm
Cues: Arms at sides, head leads; bob through water; lift head for breath; easier with fins
Notes: A tier — developmental for butterfly undulation. Easier and more natural with fins on.

**Assisted Speed Butterfly** | Tier: B
Equipment: Bungee or assisted device
Focus: Race pace feel, body position
Cues: Swim butterfly pulled by bungee; feel body position high in water; focus on timing
Notes: B tier — trains feeling of fast. Don't overuse or it throws off timing.

**Three Kicks One Pull (fly)** | Tier: B
Equipment: Fins optional
Focus: Timing, undulation balance
Cues: Dolphin kick 3 times then one full arm pull; reset rhythm between pulls
Notes: B tier — other variations teach same thing more easily. Easier with fins.

**Fly Pull with Flutter Kick** | Tier: B
Equipment: Fins optional
Focus: Pull power, recovery mechanics
Cues: Butterfly arms with flutter kick; short bursts; focus on powerful underwater pull
Notes: B tier — flattens stroke but builds pull power. Short bursts only.

**Side Breathing Butterfly** | Tier: C
Equipment: None
Focus: Head position, hip elevation
Cues: Breathe to the side instead of forward; keeps hips higher in water
Notes: C tier — 333 drill achieves same thing more effectively.

**Fly Pull with Breast Kick** | Tier: C
Equipment: None
Focus: Pull mechanics (stroke-specific)
Cues: Butterfly arms with breaststroke kick; focus on finishing the kick
Notes: C tier — situational. Better for breaststroke swimmers than butterfly specialists.

**Butterfly with Paddles** | Tier: C
Equipment: Paddles
Focus: Power feel
Cues: Full butterfly stroke with paddles
Notes: C tier — injury risk, advanced only. Not recommended for regular use.

**Single-Arm Fly** | Tier: B
Equipment: Fins
Focus: Timing, catch
Cues: Other arm extended or at side; fins support undulation

**Fly Body Dolphin** | Tier: B
Equipment: Fins
Focus: Undulation, rhythm
Cues: Arms at sides, full body dolphin on stomach; wave starts at chest not hips

## IM

**IM Reverse** | Tier: B
Equipment: None
Focus: Aerobic warm-up, stroke variety
Cues: Swim IM in reverse order: free → back → breast → fly
Notes: Common warm-up tool.

## Any

**Silent Swimming** | Tier: S
Equipment: None
Focus: Feel for the water / mental engagement
Cues: Engage all senses: listen for quiet entry, feel the catch, minimise splash
Notes: S+ in video. Use at end of workout to reinforce efficiency. Any level, no equipment.

**Kick in Streamline** | Tier: A
Equipment: Fins optional
Focus: Body position, kick mechanics
Cues: Push off wall in streamline, kick on front or back; hips high, eyes down
Notes: Trains correct body position as it mirrors actual swimming. Use instead of kickboard kick.

**Resistance Swimming** | Tier: A
Equipment: Parachute or power tower
Focus: Power, stroke-specific strength
Cues: Swim at race pace against resistance; maintain technique under load
Notes: A tier. Train shorter durations. Isolates power production. Applies to breast, fly, and other strokes.
